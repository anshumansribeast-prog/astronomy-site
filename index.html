The numbers that don't fit in your head

Space is mostly the distance between things. These four are worth sitting with.

1.3 million

Earths would fit inside the Sun. And the Sun is an average star — there are far larger ones in our own galaxy.

4.24 light-years

To Proxima Centauri, the nearest star after the Sun. Voyager 1, our fastest probe, would need roughly 73,000 years to get there.

100 billion

Stars in the Milky Way, give or take. And roughly two trillion galaxies in the observable universe.

93 billion ly

Across the observable universe. Space expanded, so the edge is farther than the universe is old in years.

5,500+

Confirmed planets around other stars, and the count is still climbing every observing season.
