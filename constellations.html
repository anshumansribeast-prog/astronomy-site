Before you go outside

Four things that make stargazing work

🌑

Wait for dark eyes

Your eyes need about 20–30 minutes to fully adapt. One glance at a phone screen resets the clock. Use a red light if you need one.

🏙️

Escape the glow

City lighting washes out all but the brightest stars. Even a short trip away from streetlights can multiply how many you can see.

🌗

Mind the Moon

A full Moon is a floodlight. For faint stars and the Milky Way, go out near a new Moon — check the Moon page to find one.

👁️

Look slightly away

The edges of your vision are more light-sensitive than the centre. To see a faint object better, look just beside it. It's called averted vision.
