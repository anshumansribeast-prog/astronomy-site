Worth knowing

Why the planets split into groups

🪨

Rocky planets

Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars formed close to the young Sun, where it was too hot for ice to survive. Only heavy rock and metal could condense, so these worlds ended up small, dense and solid.

🌪️

Gas giants

Beyond the "frost line", ice survived and planets grew huge fast. Jupiter and Saturn got big enough to hold onto hydrogen and helium — the lightest and most common elements in the universe. They have no solid surface to stand on.

🧊

Ice giants

Uranus and Neptune formed further out and later, so they captured much less hydrogen. They are mostly water, ammonia and methane — and it is the methane that makes them look blue.
